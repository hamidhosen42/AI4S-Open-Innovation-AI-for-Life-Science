# Data Guidelines

## AI4S Open Innovation: Artificial Intelligence for Life Science
### AI + Organ-on-a-Chip: Open Innovation Challenge for In Vitro Life Systems

This challenge adopts an **open data format**. Teams may use any suitable data sources, including public, private, collaborative, simulated, or synthetic data. Data compliance, transparency, and reproducibility should be ensured. Solutions validated on real OoC (organ-on-a-chip) experimental data are strongly encouraged.

No official dataset is provided or required for this challenge. The datasets listed below are examples only, provided to help teams get started. Teams may select other datasets based on their research goals, and must comply with all relevant licenses and usage conditions of whichever data they choose to use.

## Example Datasets / Resources

| Dataset / Resource | Link | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CellNet | [https://cellnet.hms.harvard.edu/](https://cellnet.hms.harvard.edu/) | A resource for cell identity analysis based on transcriptomics and gene regulatory networks. |
| BBBC | [https://bbbc.broadinstitute.org/](https://bbbc.broadinstitute.org/) | A microscopy image benchmark collection with images, annotations, and biological labels. |
| IDR | [https://idr.openmicroscopy.org/](https://idr.openmicroscopy.org/) | A public repository of biological images, phenotypes, and experimental metadata from published studies. |
| RxRx1 | [https://www.rxrx.ai/rxrx1](https://www.rxrx.ai/rxrx1) | A large-scale cellular microscopy dataset with multi-channel fluorescence images and genetic perturbation data. |
| CytoData | [https://www.cytodata.org/](https://www.cytodata.org/) | A community resource for image-based profiling, Cell Painting, and cellular phenotype analysis. |
| JUMP Hub | [https://broadinstitute.github.io/jump_hub/](https://broadinstitute.github.io/jump_hub/) | A resource portal providing datasets, documentation, tools, and analysis examples for JUMP projects. |
| JUMP-Cell Painting | [https://jump-cellpainting.broadinstitute.org/](https://jump-cellpainting.broadinstitute.org/) | A large-scale Cell Painting dataset containing compound and genetic perturbation imaging data. |

## Data Compliance

Teams must not use unauthorized personal, clinical, or restricted data. Data licenses, privacy protection, and ethical compliance should be clearly stated in the technical report.
